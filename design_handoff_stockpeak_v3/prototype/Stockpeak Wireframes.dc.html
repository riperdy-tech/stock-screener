Review & suggestions
1aWhat I'd change before building
Structure
1. Make the funnel the spine of the Desk. The five steps and the six groups (Research now → Disqualified) are the same thing; one should navigate the other.
2. Health as a drawer from the dot, not a /status route. It's a "is anything broken?" glance; a page adds a click.
3. Fold On-demand into the stock page template with an "outside the book" banner; keep the list under ⋯.
4. Track record gets its own route. It's question 3 of 3 — a tab under Desk undersells it.
Honesty in the layout
5. The headline sentence on the Desk should be phase-driven data, so "0 pass the gate" is the first thing a visitor reads today (Phase A).
6. Scoreboard leads with graded vs pending counts; returns sit below them, smaller.
7. Blocked rows: verdict word grey + reason chip on the row itself. Never a green word in a grey row (DDI fixture is undervalued but blocked — the test case).
8. Kelly 24.75 % (DDI) only appears as "upper bound" inside the size table.
Gaps found
9. Repo has BandStrip built but unused — reinstate it as the hero cell (agrees with 06).
10. Compare lens needs a home: I'd make it a 2-axis matrix (screen band × verdict) — the clearest "where they split".
11. Data requests to raise now: changes.json, depth_progress.json, record_epoch, per-ticker verdict history.
Open question: is the first-time visitor or the operator the default audience for "/"? It decides 1b vs 1d.
